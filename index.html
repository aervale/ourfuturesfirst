      Count the doublings before you graduate

      METR measures the length of task an AI system can finish on its own. From 2019 to 2025 that length doubled about every seven months. Its November 2026 update puts the pace at about every 110 days, and the best public models at up to 27 hours on one set of its tasks. Each mark below is one doubling.

      Why pacing

      Understanding takes time

      A degree takes four years. A proof takes months to check. Results now arrive faster than people can absorb them.

      No one can slow down alone

      Each company moves fast because its rivals do. Each country does the same. A pace has to be agreed across borders.

      The builders are asking too

      In July 2026, more than 1,100 employees of OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and Meta asked the U.S. government to support an international effort to build tools for pacing AI development. Pacing the Frontier
