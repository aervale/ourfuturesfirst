            About

            What is this?

            An open letter on AI from people at U.S. universities. The letter isn't finished. This form asks what you think should be in it and tells us who wants to sign when it's ready.

            Why isn't the letter out now?

            We want it to reach as many people as possible on the day it comes out, so we're gathering interest first. Please pass this along.

            Then why publish a draft?

            So you can see where it's heading. The current wording is here, with the date it last changed. It'll keep changing until launch day.

            What happens on launch day?

            Everyone on the list gets an email at the same time and can sign. The letter and its signatures are then published.

            Who can take part?

            Anyone with a U.S. university email: undergraduates, graduate students, postdocs, faculty and staff.

            Who is behind this?

            It was started by students at MIT. It's run entirely by students.

            What is a campus organizer?

            Someone who has offered to help with the letter at their university. A university can have several.

            If you volunteer, we connect you with other groups of campus organizers at your university, which means sharing your name with them, and contact you by phone or text about next steps. Organizers are listed by name on the public list and marked as organizers.

            What happens to my information?

            We use your email just to confirm your affiliation with your university and to tell you when the letter is ready. Your first name and last initial are on the public list only if you choose to show them. Poll answers are shown as totals. Your answer to “What concerns you most about AI?” isn't displayed publicly.

            The letter, so far

            Working draft. Last updated November 24, 2026.

            We study, teach and do research at American universities, and we ask our government to slow the race toward AI smarter than humans until it can be shown to be safe and under human control.

            This is where the letter stands today. It'll change as answers come in from the form.

            Signing up doesn't put your name on it. On launch day you'll see the final text and decide then whether to sign.
