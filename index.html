        About

        What is this?

        An open letter on AI from people at American universities. It is not finished yet. This site is the first stage: a short form that asks what you think, and collects the email of everyone who wants to hear when the letter is ready to sign.

        Why isn't the letter out now?

        Because a letter that trickles out gets ignored. We are collecting interest first, so that on the day it is released it reaches as many people as possible at once.

        That only works if this gets passed along, so we are counting on you to help. Thank you.

        Then why publish a draft?

        So you know what you are signing up for. The current wording is here, with the date it was last changed. It will keep changing until launch day, and nobody signs anything until they have read the final version.

        What happens on launch day?

        Everyone on the list hears from us at the same moment and can sign. The letter and its signatures are then published and announced publicly.

        Who can take part?

        Anyone with a U.S. university email who is part of campus life: undergraduates, graduate students, postdocs, faculty and staff.

        Who is behind this?

        It was started by students at MIT and is spreading to other universities from person to person. It is run by students.

        What is a campus organizer?

        Someone who has offered to help with the letter at their own university. There is no single organizer per campus: a university can have several, and the more the better.

        If you volunteer, we connect you with the other campus organizers at your university and start a group chat with all of you, so you can coordinate. That means your name is shared with them. We then contact you about what comes next. Organizers who show their name are also marked on the public list, so other people at their university can see who is involved.

        We reach organizers by phone or text, which is why we ask for a number and your consent to texts. You can untick the box at any time.

        What happens to my information?

        Your email is used to confirm you belong to a university and to contact you about the letter. Your name appears on the public list only if you choose to show it. Poll answers are shown only as totals. Your answer to “What concerns you most about AI?” will not be displayed publicly.

        The letter, so far

        Working draft. Last updated November 24, 2026.

        We study, teach and do research at American universities, and we ask our government to slow the race toward AI smarter than humans until it can be shown to be safe and under human control.

        This is where the letter stands today. It will change as answers come in from the form.

        Signing up does not put your name on this. On launch day you will see the final text and decide then whether to sign.
