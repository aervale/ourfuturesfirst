            About

            What is this?

            An open letter on AI from people at U.S. universities. The letter isn't finished. This form asks what you think should be in it and tells us who wants to sign when it's ready.

            Why isn't the letter out now?

            We want it to reach as many people as possible on the day it comes out, so we're gathering interest first. Please pass this along.

            Then why publish a draft?

            So you can see where it's heading. The current wording is here, with the date it last changed. It'll keep changing until launch day.

            What happens on launch day?

            Everyone on the list gets an email at the same time and can sign. The letter and its signatures are then published.

            Who can take part?

            Anyone with a U.S. university email: undergraduates, graduate students, postdocs, faculty and staff.

            Who is behind this?

            It was started by students at MIT. It's run entirely by students.

            What is a campus organizer?

            Someone who has offered to help with the letter at their university. A university can have several.

            If you volunteer, we connect you with other groups of campus organizers at your university, which means sharing your name with them, and contact you by phone or text about next steps. Organizers are listed by name on the public list and marked as organizers.

            What happens to my information?

            We use your email just to confirm your affiliation with your university and to tell you when the letter is ready. Your first name and last initial are on the public list only if you choose to show them. Poll answers are shown as totals. Your answer to “What concerns you most about AI?” isn't displayed publicly.

            Contact

              Privacy

              Last updated November 24, 2026.

              Who we are

              Our Futures First is a project run by students at U.S. universities. It is collecting interest in an open letter on AI. You can reach us at ourfuturesfirst@gmail.com.

              What we collect

              What you enter in the form: your name, your university email, your role and year, your major or field, a social media handle if you choose to give one, a phone number if you volunteer as a campus organizer, your answers to the six questions, your written answer, and whether you offered to help.

              We also record whether and when you confirmed your email. The services that host this website and its database receive your IP address and basic browser details when your browser loads the page. A code saved in your browser lets you reopen your own entry from the same device. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

              What is public

              If you choose to show your name, it appears on the public list with your role and university. If you volunteered as a campus organizer, the list says so. Answers to the six questions are shown only as totals, and each university is shown with its number of sign-ups.

              Your email, phone number, social media handle and written answer are never shown.

              How we use it

              To confirm that you belong to a U.S. university, to contact you about the letter, to count sign-ups and show totals, and to decide what goes in the letter. If you volunteer as a campus organizer, your name is shared with the other campus organizers at your university.

              Text messages

              A phone number is optional. We text you only if you give a number; adding it is your agreement to be texted by Our Futures First. Texts are about the letter and organizing on your campus. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop at any time, or HELP for help.

              We do not sell phone numbers or text-message consent, and we do not share them with anyone for their own marketing.

              Who we share it with

              Only the services that run the site for us: the company that hosts this website, Supabase for the database and sign-in emails, and an email delivery service. Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and code to your browser and so see your IP address. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

              How long we keep it

              Until the letter project ends, or until you ask us to delete it. An entry whose email is never confirmed is not counted or shown.

              Your choices

              If you list fellow students to share the letter with, we keep those addresses with your sign-up and use them only to send those people the letter. We do not show them or pass them on.

              You can change your entry from the browser you signed up in, or from the link we emailed you. To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email ourfuturesfirst@gmail.com.

              Do Not Track

              We do not track you across other websites, so this site behaves the same way whatever your browser's Do Not Track setting is.

              Children

              This site is for people at universities. It is not directed to children under 13.

              Changes

              If this page changes, we will update the date at the top.
